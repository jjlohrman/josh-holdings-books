# Privacy Policy (draft for Intuit app questionnaire)

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This private application ("the App") is used only by Josh's companies, Rosario RE LLC and Villamil RE LLC.

What we access: accounting data in the companies' own QuickBooks Online files (accounts, transactions, reports), after the owner authorizes it through Intuit's sign-in.
How we use it: only for internal bookkeeping, reconciliation, and tax preparation for those companies.
Sharing: we do not sell or share the data with third parties, other than the companies' own accountants when the owner directs it.
Storage and security: access tokens are stored encrypted or in restricted-permission files on a private server and are never published. Data is fetched on demand and not kept longer than needed.
Revoking access: disconnect the App in QuickBooks Online (Settings, Apps) at any time; tokens then stop working.
